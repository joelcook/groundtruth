# Programs & Services (Source Snapshot)

Maplewood runs year-round programs for every age. The structured rows are in
`sources/facts/library-facts.yaml`.

- **Summer Reading Program** — all ages, summer, registration required.
- **After-School Homework Help** — drop-in tutoring for kids on school days.
- **ESL Conversation Circle** — weekly adult English practice, drop-in.
- **One-on-One Tech Help** — 45-minute appointments for help with devices and apps.

The eLibrary app (ebooks and audiobooks) is available to all cardholders.
