# Locations & Hours (Frozen Scrape)

Maplewood Public Library has three branches: **Central** (downtown), **Eastside**,
and **Lakeview**. Central keeps the longest hours, including evenings and a short
Sunday afternoon; Eastside is open daily except Sunday; Lakeview is open Tuesday
through Saturday.

Exact opening and closing times per branch and per day are maintained as
structured facts in `sources/facts/library-facts.yaml` and served through the
`get_hours` tool. They are never written into wiki prose.
