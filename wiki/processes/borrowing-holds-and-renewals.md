# Borrowing, Holds, and Renewals

Once you have a [[getting-a-library-card|library card]], borrowing is
self-service. The exact loan length and how many times you can renew depend on
the item type and are always shown on the item's page in the catalog and in
**My Account** — check there rather than guessing.

## Checking Out

Bring your items and your card to a self-checkout station or the circulation
desk. Most physical items check out on the spot. For digital items, see
[[using-the-elibrary-app|the eLibrary app]].

## Placing a Hold

If an item is out or lives at another branch, place a **hold** and the library
will route it to your pickup branch:

1. Find the item in the online catalog.
2. Choose **Place Hold** and pick your pickup branch.
3. You will be notified when it arrives, and it will wait for you on the holds
   shelf for several days before being released to the next person.

Holds are the easiest way to gather materials from across all three branches in
one place.

## Renewing

Items renew automatically unless someone else is waiting or you have hit the
renewal limit. You can also renew manually from **My Account**. If an item will
not renew, it is usually because there is a hold behind it.

> [!note] We are fine-free
> Maplewood does not charge daily overdue fines. You will not rack up a balance
> for a late book. You are still responsible for returning items and for the
> cost of anything lost or damaged — the details, and any current charges, are
> in [[fines-and-fees]] (amounts come from `get_fee`).
