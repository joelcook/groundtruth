# Summer Reading Program

Summer Reading is the library's signature challenge, and it is for **everyone** —
there are separate tracks for kids, teens, and adults. You read, log what you
finish, and earn prizes and entries into drawings along the way.

## How It Works

1. **Sign up.** Registration is required so we can track your progress and
   prizes; the assistant can confirm with `get_program` (look for
   `summer-reading`). You can register at any branch or online.
2. **Read anything.** Books, audiobooks, ebooks from
   [[using-the-elibrary-app|the eLibrary app]] — it all counts.
3. **Log your reading.** Use the reading app or a paper log from the desk.
4. **Earn prizes.** Hit milestones to collect rewards and drawing entries.

## When

The program runs over the summer, and it opens with a kickoff event. Exact dates
are facts, so ask the assistant for them with `get_event` (the **Summer Reading
Kickoff**) and `get_program` for the overall schedule, rather than trusting a
date written on this page.

## Why Bother

Beyond prizes, Summer Reading keeps kids' skills sharp over the break and gives
adults a nudge to read for pleasure. It is free, and everything you read on
[[using-the-elibrary-app|your phone]] counts toward your total.
