# Trash and recycling

Find your zone by address on the city website. Zone A: Monday trash,
recycling every other Monday. Zone B: Wednesday. Zone C: Friday. Put carts at
the curb by 6 a.m. with lids closed. Recycling takes paper, cardboard, metal
cans, and plastic bottles. No plastic bags. Holiday weeks shift one day later.
