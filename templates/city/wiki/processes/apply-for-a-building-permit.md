# Apply for a building permit

**You need a permit for** new construction, additions, structural changes, new
electrical or plumbing, decks, and tall fences.

1. **Apply online** with a site plan and drawings.
2. **Wait for plan review.** It usually takes a couple of weeks.
3. **Schedule inspections** after approval, before you cover any work.

The assistant looks up the current permit fee with `get_fee` and the permit
office's contact details with `get_office`.
