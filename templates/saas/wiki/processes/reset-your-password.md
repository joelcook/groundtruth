# Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your work email.
3. Open the link we email you and pick a new password. The link expires
   quickly, so use it right away.

**Using single sign-on?** If your company signs in through an identity
provider (Okta, Google, Microsoft), reset your password there instead. We
never see SSO passwords.

**Locked out?** Too many failed attempts locks the account for a short while.
Wait, then try again or reset your password.
