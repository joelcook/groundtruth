# Refunds and cancellation

**Annual plans can be refunded** if you ask within the refund window. The
assistant looks up the exact window with `get_limit` so it's always current.

**Monthly plans aren't refundable**, but you can cancel any time and keep
access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

## How to request a refund

Contact support with your workspace name (the assistant can find the right
channel with `get_support`).
