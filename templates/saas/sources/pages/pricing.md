# Pricing

- **Starter**: $9 per user per month. 3 projects, 10 GB storage.
- **Team**: $29 per user per month. Unlimited projects, 100 GB storage.
- **Enterprise**: custom annual pricing. Unlimited everything, SSO, audit logs.

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial, no card required.
Files up to 2 GB each can be uploaded on any plan.
