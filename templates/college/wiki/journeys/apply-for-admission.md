# Apply for admission

1. **Submit the online application.** It's free.
2. **Send your transcript.** Your final high school transcript or GED.
3. **Apply for financial aid** by completing the FAFSA, if you want aid.
4. **Meet an advisor** once you're admitted, to pick your classes.
5. **Register online** when registration opens. The assistant looks up the
   exact date with `get_deadline`.

Questions about your application go to admissions (contact details come from
`get_office`).
