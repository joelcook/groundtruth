# Drop or withdraw from a class

**Dropping** before the drop deadline removes the class from your record and
refunds the tuition. **Withdrawing** after that deadline gives you a W grade
and no refund. The assistant looks up both deadlines with `get_deadline`.

## How

Both happen online in the student portal. If you receive financial aid, talk
to financial aid first: dropping below full-time can change your aid.
