# Dropping vs. withdrawing

Dropping a class before the drop deadline removes it from your record and
refunds tuition. Withdrawing after the drop deadline gives you a W grade and no
refund. Both are done online in the student portal. Talk to financial aid
first if you receive aid, because dropping below full-time can change it.
