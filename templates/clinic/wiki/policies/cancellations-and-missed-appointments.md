# Cancellations and missed appointments

**Cancel or reschedule at least a day ahead** through the patient portal or by
phone. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged a fee; the assistant looks
up the current amount with `get_price`.

Repeated missed appointments may mean we ask you to find another provider.
