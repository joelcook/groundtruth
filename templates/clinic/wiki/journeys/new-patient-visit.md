# Your first visit as a new patient

1. **Book** online or by phone (the assistant looks up the location's phone
   with `get_location`).
2. **Fill out intake forms** in the patient portal before you come.
3. **Bring** a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of your medications.
4. **Arrive a little early** for your first visit.

Not sure we take your insurance? The assistant checks with `get_insurance`.
