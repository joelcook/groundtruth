# Public Computers, WiFi, and Printing

Every branch has free public computers and free WiFi. You do not need to be a
cardholder to use the WiFi; logging in to a public computer uses your
[[getting-a-library-card|library card]] number and PIN.

## Public Computers

Sign in at any open computer with your card number and PIN. Sessions have a time
limit so everyone gets a turn; if no one is waiting, you can usually extend.
Guests without a card can ask staff for a day pass.

## WiFi

The network is open and free during open hours — check `get_hours` for the
branch you are visiting. The signal reaches the parking lot at most branches.

## Printing and Copying

You can print from a library computer or from your own device. Printing is
**not free**, but it is inexpensive; black-and-white and color are priced
differently. Ask the assistant for the **current per-page price with `get_fee`**
(`printing-bw` and `printing-color`) instead of relying on a number written
here. Pay at the print-release station or the desk.

> [!tip] Scanning is free
> Scanning to email or to a USB drive does not cost anything. Only printed and
> copied pages are charged.

## Need Help?

Book a session of [[summer-reading-program|one-on-one tech help]] for hands-on
guidance, or reach the reference desk through `get_contact`.
