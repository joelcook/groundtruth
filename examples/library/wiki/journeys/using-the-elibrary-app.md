# Borrowing eBooks and Audiobooks

Your [[getting-a-library-card|library card]] comes with a full digital library
you can use from your phone or tablet through the **eLibrary app** — no trip to a
branch required.

## Get Set Up

1. Install the eLibrary app from your device's app store.
2. Choose **Maplewood Public Library** when it asks for your library.
3. Sign in with your **card number and PIN** (the same ones you set up at the
   desk).

## Borrowing

- Search or browse, then tap **Borrow**. The title downloads to your device.
- Titles return themselves at the end of the loan, so there is nothing late and
  nothing to bring back — which pairs nicely with our [[fines-and-fees|fine-free
  policy]] anyway.
- If a popular title is checked out, place a **hold** just like with
  [[borrowing-holds-and-renewals|physical items]] and you will be notified when
  it is ready.

## Reading and Listening

The app reads ebooks and plays audiobooks in one place, remembers where you left
off across devices, and works offline once a title is downloaded. Everything you
finish here counts toward the [[summer-reading-program|Summer Reading Program]].

> [!tip] Trouble signing in?
> The most common snag is a forgotten PIN. Reset it at any branch or by calling
> the circulation desk — find the number with `get_contact`.
