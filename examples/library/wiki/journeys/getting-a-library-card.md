# How to Get a Library Card

A library card is free for anyone who lives, works, or goes to school in the
Maplewood area. It unlocks borrowing, the public computers, and the whole
[[using-the-elibrary-app|digital collection]].

## What You Need

Bring one item that shows your **name** and one that shows your **current
address** — a photo ID plus a piece of mail is the usual combination. If your ID
already shows your current address, that single item is enough. Children can get
a card with a parent or guardian present to sign.

## Where to Sign Up

You can register in person at the circulation desk of any branch. Hours differ
by location, so check before you go — ask the assistant for `get_hours`, and use
`get_contact` for the circulation desk phone number if you want to call ahead.

If you would rather start online, you can pre-register on the library website and
finish at any desk; you only need to show your documents once.

## After You Have a Card

- Your first card is free. There is a small charge to replace a lost card — the
  assistant can quote the current amount with `get_fee`.
- Set a PIN at the desk. You will need your card number and PIN to log in to the
  catalog and to [[using-the-elibrary-app|the eLibrary app]].
- You are responsible for items checked out on your card, so report a lost card
  promptly. See [[fines-and-fees]] for how lost and overdue items are handled.

> [!tip] No proof of address?
> Students, people experiencing homelessness, and new arrivals can still get a
> card — talk to the circulation desk about alternative verification. Reach them
> through `get_contact`.
