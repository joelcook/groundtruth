# Borrowing Policy (Current)

Maplewood Public Library cards are free to anyone who lives, works, or attends
school in the service area. A first card is free; a replacement card carries a
small fee.

## Getting a Card

Bring one item that shows your name and one that shows your current address; a
photo ID plus a piece of mail is the usual combination, and an ID that already
shows your current address is enough on its own. Children can get a card with a
parent or guardian present to sign. Register in person at the circulation desk of
any branch, or pre-register on the library website and finish at any desk; you
show your documents once.

Students, people experiencing homelessness, and new arrivals without proof of
address can still get a card: the circulation desk offers alternative
verification.

At the desk you set a PIN. Your card number and PIN sign you in to the catalog,
My Account, the public computers, and the eLibrary app. A forgotten PIN can be
reset at any branch or by calling the circulation desk. You are responsible for
items checked out on your card, so report a lost card promptly.

## Checking Out, Holds, and Renewals

Check out at a self-checkout station or at the circulation desk. Loan lengths and
renewal limits depend on the item type and are shown on each item's page in the
catalog and in My Account. My Account also lists your holds and shows when one is
ready to pick up, and staff at any branch's circulation desk can help with
checkouts, holds, and renewals.

If an item is checked out or kept at another branch, place a hold: find it in the
online catalog, choose Place Hold, and pick your pickup branch. We notify you when
it arrives, and it waits on the holds shelf for several days before it goes to
the next person. Holds are the easiest way to gather items from all three
branches in one place.

Items renew automatically unless someone else is waiting for them or you have
reached the renewal limit. You can also renew from My Account. An item that won't
renew usually has a hold behind it.

## Overdue Items — Fine-Free

The Library Board eliminated daily overdue fines in 2024. **We no longer charge
overdue fines.** Patrons remain responsible for the replacement cost of items
that are lost or returned damaged. Returning a late item in good condition
settles the account with no charge. A replacement charge for a lost item is
removed if you later find it and return it in good condition.

This policy supersedes all earlier fee schedules, including printed flyers
distributed before 2024.

## Other Charges

Printing and photocopying are charged per page. Meeting rooms may be reserved for
a fee, except for nonprofit community groups, which use them free of charge.

| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Black-and-white printing and copies | $0.10 per page |
| Color printing and copies | $0.50 per page |
| Replacement library card (your first card is free) | $2.00 per card |
| Meeting or study room (free for nonprofit community groups) | $25.00 per hour |
| Overdue fines | $0.00 per day: Maplewood is fine-free |
