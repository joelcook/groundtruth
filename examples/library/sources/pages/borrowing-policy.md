# Borrowing Policy (Current)

Maplewood Public Library cards are free to anyone who lives, works, or attends
school in the service area. A first card is free; a replacement card carries a
small fee.

## Overdue Items — Fine-Free

The Library Board eliminated daily overdue fines in 2024. **We no longer charge
overdue fines.** Patrons remain responsible for the replacement cost of items
that are lost or returned damaged. Returning a late item in good condition
settles the account with no charge.

This policy supersedes all earlier fee schedules, including printed flyers
distributed before 2024.

## Other Charges

Printing and photocopying are charged per page. Meeting rooms may be reserved for
a fee, except for nonprofit community groups, which use them free of charge.

| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Black-and-white printing and copies | $0.10 per page |
| Color printing and copies | $0.50 per page |
| Replacement library card (your first card is free) | $2.00 per card |
| Meeting or study room (free for nonprofit community groups) | $25.00 per hour |
| Overdue fines | $0.00 per day: Maplewood is fine-free |
