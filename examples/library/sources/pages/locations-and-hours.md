# Locations & Hours

Maplewood Public Library has three branches: **Central** (downtown), **Eastside**,
and **Lakeview**. Central keeps the longest hours, including evenings and a short
Sunday afternoon; Eastside is open daily except Sunday; Lakeview is open Tuesday
through Saturday.

## Maplewood Central Library (downtown)

100 Library Plaza, Maplewood · 555-0100

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. |
| Friday | 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. |
| Saturday | 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. |
| Sunday | 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m. |
