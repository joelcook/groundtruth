# Programs & Services

Maplewood runs year-round programs for every age, and every program is free.

- **Summer Reading Program**: all ages, June through August, registration
  required. Read, log books, and earn prizes, with tracks for kids, teens, and
  adults. It's free. Register at any branch or online; books, audiobooks, and
  eBooks from the eLibrary app all count. Log your reading in the reading app or
  on a paper log from the desk, and reach milestones for prizes and drawing
  entries. The program opens with a kickoff event, and it keeps kids' reading
  skills sharp over the summer break.
- **After-School Homework Help**: drop-in tutoring from volunteers for
  elementary and middle schoolers, on school-year weekdays after school.
- **ESL Conversation Circle**: informal English conversation practice for adult
  learners, weekly on Thursday evenings, drop-in.
- **One-on-One Tech Help**: book a 45-minute appointment for help with devices,
  email, or apps.

## The eLibrary App

The eLibrary app (ebooks and audiobooks) is free for all cardholders. Install
it from your device's app store, choose Maplewood Public Library, and sign in with
your card number and PIN. Search or browse and tap Borrow to download a title;
titles return themselves at the end of the loan, so nothing is ever late. If a
title is checked out, place a hold and you're notified when it's ready. The app
reads ebooks and plays audiobooks in one place, remembers your place across
devices, and works offline once a title is downloaded.
